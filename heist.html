Brewing Guide:

Drip / Filter

Coffee: Water Ratio:
1:14.5
Make time:
5 min
Best served:
Hot · makes about 4 cups
Grind:
Medium, like table salt · C40 26-28

Ingredients:

  • Filtered water 1000 g
  • Heist Coffee 69 g

Smooth Talker

Medium Roast

Shop 1/4 caf

How to make:

  1. Grind to a medium, table-salt texture. Decaf throws more fines than caffeinated coffee, so if the brew stalls, go coarser before you change anything else.
  2. Rinse a flat-bottom paper filter with hot water and discard the rinse water.
  3. Add the grounds and level the bed flat. An uneven bed is the most common cause of a weak pot.
  4. If your brewer has a pre-infusion setting, use it.
  5. Brew. Most machines take four to six minutes on their own.
  6. Stir the carafe before you pour. Batch brews stratify, and the first cup and the last cup are different coffees otherwise.
  7. Drink within 30 minutes. Thermal carafe, never a hot plate.

Espresso Martini

Best made with:
Smooth Talker
Make time:
3 min
Best served:
Cold · up · coupe
Grind:
Fine (espresso)

Ingredients:

  • Vodka 2 oz
  • Coffee Liqueur 0.5 oz
  • Smooth Talker espresso, fresh 1 oz (a double, 38-40 g)
  • Demerara syrup (2:1)
  • Coffee beans, to garnish 3

Smooth Talker

Medium Roast

Shop 1/4 caf

How to make:

  1. Chill the coupe.
  2. Pull the espresso straight into the shaker.
  3. Add the syrup and a full scoop of ice.
  4. Shake as hard as you can for 15 seconds.
  5. Fine strain into the chilled coupe.

It should land with a dense, hazelnut-coloured foam on top.

Pour Over

Coffee: Water Ratio:
1:16
Make time:
4 min
Best served:
Hot · 10 oz
Grind:
Medium-fine, like coarse sand · C40 20-22

Ingredients:

  • Smooth Talker, whole bean 20 g
  • Filtered water 320 g

Smooth Talker

Medium Roast

Shop 1/4 caf

How to make:

  1. Boil, then let the kettle rest 30 seconds. You want 200-204 F / 94-96 C.
  2. Rinse a V60 paper thoroughly and discard the rinse water. This is what removes the paper taste and preheats the brewer.
  3. Add the grounds, tap the bed flat, and make a shallow well in the centre.
  4. Bloom with 50 g of water. Swirl gently, then wait 45 seconds.
  5. At 0:45, pour steadily to 200 g.
  6. At 1:30, pour to 320 g. Keep the stream central and off the walls.
  7. Swirl once to level the bed, then leave it alone.
  8. Drawdown should finish between 2:45 and 3:00. Past 3:30, grind coarser next time.

Espresso

Coffee: Water Ratio:
1:2.1
Make time:
30 sec
Best served:
Hot · straight or in milk
Grind:
Fine · 2-3 steps coarser than your usual medium roast

Ingredients:

  • Smooth Talker, ground 18 g
  • Yield in the cup 38-40 g

Smooth Talker

Medium Roast

Shop 1/4 caf

How to make:

  1. Set the brew temperature to 200 F / 92-93 C. Lower than you would run a caffeinated medium roast.
  2. Break up the clumps with a needle tool. Decaf pucks channel more than caffeinated ones, so this step is not optional.
  3. Level, then tamp straight and light.
  4. Pre-infuse for five to eight seconds, then run the shot.
  5. Stop at 38-40 g in the cup, around 27 to 30 seconds total.
  6. If the shot gushes or runs pale early, the puck channeled. Redistribute and re-tamp. Do not grind finer to chase it.

Affogato

Make time:
1 min
Best served:
Cold glass, immediately
Grind:
Fine (espresso)

Ingredients:

  • Vanilla gelato, high butterfat 85-100 g
  • Smooth Talker espresso 38-40 g
  • Flaky sea salt a pinch

Smooth Talker

Medium Roast

Shop 1/4 caf

How to make:

  1. Put the glass in the freezer for a few minutes. A cold glass is what stops it turning to soup.
  2. Scoop the gelato in. Use the highest butterfat you can find — cheap ice cream goes watery the second the espresso hits it.
  3. Pull the espresso and pour it straight over, at the table.
  4. Add a pinch of flaky salt. Optional, and also the best version.
  5. Serve immediately, with a spoon.

Cold Brew

Coffee: Water Ratio:
1:8 concentrate
Make time:
12 hr steep
Best served:
Cold · over ice · diluted 1:1
Grind:
Coarse, coarser than French press · C40 32-34

Ingredients:

  • Smooth Talker, whole bean 150 g
  • Cold filtered water 1200 g
  • Fine sea salt a pinch

Smooth Talker

Medium Roast

Shop 1/4 caf

How to make:

  1. Grind coarse, coarser than you would for French press. Fines make cold brew muddy and they never settle out.
  2. Combine the grounds and cold filtered water in a large jar.
  3. Stir once, only until every ground is wet. Then leave it alone, stirring mid-steep pulls bitterness.
  4. Steep 12 to 14 hours at room temperature, or 18 to 20 in the fridge. Decaf extracts faster than caffeinated coffee, so pull it earlier than a recipe you have used before.
  5. Strain through a fine mesh.
  6. Strain a second time through a paper filter or a clean cloth, and do not squeeze the bed. Two-stage filtering is the whole difference between cafe and home cold brew.
  7. Add a two-finger pinch of fine salt per litre. It kills the residual bitterness and reads as sweetness. Try it before you dismiss it.
  8. Dilute 1:1 with water, milk or oat milk over ice. Keeps 10 to 14 days sealed in the fridge.

AeroPress

Coffee: Water Ratio:
1:15
Make time:
2 min
Best served:
Hot · 8 oz
Grind:
Medium-fine · C40 18-20

Ingredients:

  • Smooth Talker, whole bean 16 g

Smooth Talker

Medium Roast

Shop 1/4 caf

How to make:

  1. Boil and rest the kettle to 200 F / 92-93 C.
  2. Rinse one paper filter, cap it, and stand the AeroPress on your mug the normal way up. Use two papers if you want a cleaner cup.
  3. Add the grounds, then pour all the water in one go, right to the top.
  4. Stir for 10 seconds.
  5. Cap it and steep until 1:30 on the clock.
  6. Press slowly for 30 seconds. Stop the moment you hear the hiss, pushing past it only forces fines through.

French Press

Coffee: Water Ratio:
1:15
Make time:
9 min
Grind:
Coarse, like rough breadcrumbs · C40 30-32

Ingredients:

  • Smooth Talker, whole bean 30 g
  • Filtered water 450 g

Smooth Talker

Medium Roast

Shop 1/4 caf

How to make:

  1. Boil and rest the kettle to 200-203 F / 93-95 C.
  2. Add the grounds, pour all the water, and start a timer.
  3. At 4:00, break the crust with three gentle stirs.
  4. Skim the foam and floating grounds off the top and throw them away.
  5. Rest the plunger on the surface but do not press. Wait five more minutes — the fines settle on their own.
  6. Press just to the surface, then pour out every drop immediately.
  7. Never leave brewed coffee sitting on the bed. It keeps extracting and turns bitter.
